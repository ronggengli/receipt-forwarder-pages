receipt-forwarder

English
receipt-forwarder is a personal, single-user script that runs on the owner's own computer. Once a month it looks in the owner's own Gmail mailbox for two specific monthly receipt emails (a Google Play invoice and a credit card e-statement), and forwards them to a recipient chosen by the owner. It is not a public service, has no other users, and is not distributed.

See the Privacy Policy.
